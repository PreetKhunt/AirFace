## Key Metrics & Thresholds

- **MAPE $\le 5.0\%$**: Measures mean absolute percentage deviation.
- **Pearson Correlation $r \ge 0.85$**: Measures directional co-movement.
- **Temporal Alignment:** Daily scraped index averaged monthly ($\bar{I}^M$) to align with monthly reference benchmarks.
