## Recommendation

- **Tier 1 (Elementary Route Level):** Jevons Geometric Mean Index (Unweighted, robust to dynamic spikes).
- **Tier 2 (National Aggregation):** Young / Modified Laspeyres Index with DGCA Passenger Traffic Weights $w_r$.
- **Rejected:** Carli (Upward bias), Paasche/Fisher (Infeasible due to missing real-time ticket quantities $q_t$).
