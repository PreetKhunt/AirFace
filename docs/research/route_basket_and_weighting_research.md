## Key Findings

1. **Directionality:** $A \rightarrow B$ and $B \rightarrow A$ are strictly separate directional markets due to airport UDF variations, flight slot timing, and demand asymmetry.
2. **DGCA Passenger Volume Weighting:** Route weights derived from official DGCA monthly city-pair passenger traffic reports.
3. **Illustrative Basket:** Top 15 domestic corridors account for >60% of domestic passenger traffic.
